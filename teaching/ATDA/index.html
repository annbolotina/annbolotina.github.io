Group 2: Compressed Data Structures for Query Processing

This group studies compressed representations for query processing, including bitmap indexes, decision diagrams, and modern compressed and succinct data structures.

Course structure

  1. Compressed Bitmap Indexes. Bitmap queries, data characteristics and compressed bitmap formats.
  2. Decision Diagrams. Boolean functions, structural sharing and variable ordering.
  3. Modern Compressed and Succinct Data Structures. Compact representations and experimental evaluation of research papers.
